# Studies excluded from the analysis

* **Thrailkill 2022b (subset 1)**: 427 participants with 27 choices each (Kirby questionnaire). None of the models could be fitted within the available computing time; the study was removed from all models and pooled summaries. Its cleaned data are kept unchanged in `clean_data.csv.gz`.
* **Weidacker 2020** (14 participants): fitted and reported, but left out of pooled estimates (see `03_summarise.py`).
